MASK-OFF · Lesson 0002 · ~12 minutes

Diversity is two machines, not one

What the code actually does, which claim each half defends, and the command that runs it.

Terms defined before first use Facet — one labeled dimension the paper claims spread on. Two are claimed: domain and user role.
Row — one subcategory line under a domain heading in seed_subcategories.md. 14 domains × 40 rows = 560 rows.
Seed — the authored sketch (world, hidden fact, sender) that a generator expands into an item.
κ (Cohen's kappa) — agreement between two raters after removing the agreement chance alone would produce.
Hill number — an effective count of categories. q=0 counts how many options appear. q=1 is exp(Shannon entropy): "as diverse as N evenly used options".

1. The one idea

The diversity workstream is two separate machines that meet at one hinge.

MachineWhere it livesWhat it doesClaim it defends
A — Constructionmask_off/, Stage AForces spread into the corpus while it is authored"The corpus was engineered to be diverse."
B — Measurementdiversity/Labels the finished corpus and counts the spread"The corpus is diverse — here is the number."

Most confusion about this workstream comes from reading one file and assuming it belongs to the other machine. seedgen.py never measures. hill.py never generates.

The hinge Machine B produces two kinds of number, and they are not peers. κ is a gate: it says the labels mean something. Hill numbers are the result: they say the corpus is spread. A Hill number computed on labels that failed κ is not a weak result. It is not a result.

2. Machine A — four mechanisms, all implemented

Each mechanism attacks one way an LLM author collapses onto a favourite shape.

MechanismCollapse it preventsCode
Balanced row draw
Round-robin over the 14 domains, always drawing from the domain that is furthest behind. Seeded at SAMPLE_SEED = 42, and it skips rows already on disk so a top-up draws new rows.		 Domain pile-up. Without it, a human typed the row list by hand. mask_off/taxonomy.py:82 balanced_rows
Rotating family window
Nine trigger families (how the hidden fact eventually surfaces: regulator review, court/arbitration, physical event…). Each authoring call is offered a rotating 5 of the 9, and no two seeds in one call may share a family.		 Trigger monoculture. The audit found the older corpus at 84% "regulator review". mask_off/seedgen.py:71 family_window
Name pool without replacement
154 person names, shuffled, drawn 6 per call, and the stream resumes from what is already logged when a corpus is topped up.		 Entity convergence. The audit found "Priya" in 6 of 26 items. mask_off/seedgen.py:90 name_stream
Embed–flag–regenerate
Embed each seed's WORLD + FACT text. Any pair at cosine ≥ 0.90 is flagged; the later seed is rewritten with its neighbour supplied as a negative example.		 Near-duplicate seeds inside one row. mask_off/seedgen.py:719 distinct

Note what Machine A does not do: it never checks a role axis. Role spread is not engineered. It is only measured, after the fact, by Machine B.

3. Machine B — the pipeline that ends in a number

Five steps. Each one has a file, and each file has a self-check in __main__.

#StepFileOutput
1Fix the menu. Three role axes, each an ordered decision list — the rater takes the first true line.diversity/labeling/roles.pyA menu_version hash
2Freeze the sample. 300 items, stratified by domain, minimum 10 per domain, seeded, sorted, committed.diversity/labeling/sample.pysample_300.jsonl + a sample_sha
3Label twice. Both authors label the same file. An LLM judge labels it too.author_notebook.py, judge_labels.pyauthor_<initials>.jsonl
4Gate. Cohen's κ per axis, author against author, with a 2000-draw bootstrap interval.diversity/labeling/kappa.pyThree κ values
5Count. Coverage (Hill q=0), effective number (Hill q=1), evenness, joint grid.diversity/labeling/hill.pyThe paper's diversity table

Why the menu is an ordered list

This is the single most load-bearing design decision in Machine B, and it came from a failure. The first role facet was a flat list of 13 archetypes. It scored κ = 0.550 — below the 0.67 floor. The cause was not careless rating. A practice owner ordering from a lab is truthfully both "small business" and "B2B", so two careful raters split.

Prose tie-break rules sat beside the menu and could not fix it. The ordering sits inside it: the broad residual line ("seller / provider") is placed below every narrow line it would otherwise swallow. The rater never compares two true options, so what κ measures becomes disagreement about the email rather than about the menu.

Transferable rule When two raters disagree, first ask whether the instrument admits two right answers. Fix the instrument before you retrain the rater.

Three guards you should know exist

  • Stamp guard. Every saved label row carries labeler, menu_version, sample_sha, ts. The notebook refuses to append if any stamp moved, and kappa.py refuses to compare two files whose stamps disagree. A stale branch cannot silently mix two menus.
  • Overlap diagnostic. kappa.py prints the top confusion pairs per axis. A single ordered pair holding ≥ 30% of an axis's disagreements is pre-declared to be a named menu overlap, not noise. This is the hand-analysis that caught the flat-13 flaw, made automatic.
  • Cluster bootstrap. When row ids are composite (item#response), the interval resamples items, not responses. Three responses graded on one screen are one joint judgment; resampling them separately would report a falsely narrow interval.

4. What the numbers look like right now

Run today over the 209-item measurement scan:

beneficiary — coverage 5 of 5 · effective 3.51 · evenness 0.70
institution — coverage 6 of 6 · effective 4.06 · evenness 0.68
standing    — coverage 4 of 4 · effective 2.01 · evenness 0.50
joint grid  — 34 combinations of 120 · effective 20.05

Read it in three moves:

  1. Coverage is full on all three axes. Every option was used at least once. Hill q=0 is satisfied.
  2. Balance is the weak part. The standing axis has 4 options but behaves like 2 — 71% "current", and "took over" appears twice in 209 items. Evenness 0.50 says exactly that: half of what the axis could carry.
  3. The joint grid is the honest headline. 34 of 120 cells, effective 20. But the file's own footer states the caveat: some of the 120 cells are implausible rather than missing, so the reachable set must be declared before coverage of it is claimed.
Do not cite these out/scan/README.md says it in the file: the 209 items pool many pipeline variants. The numbers describe the experiments run so far, not the frozen corpus. They are a smoke test for the measurement code, and they are not a binding κ surface.

The three missing text metrics are the lexical, syntactic, and semantic rows. They answer a different objection from the categorical rows: "your scenarios all read the same". Learning record 0002 already demoted them from headline to supporting, so the categorical machinery being finished first is the plan working, not the plan slipping.

6. The procedure, as commands

Machine A, seed construction:

.venv/bin/python -m mask_off.seedgen author --seeds 300 --out experiments/<name>
.venv/bin/python -m mask_off.seedgen distinct experiments/<name>/scenarios/seeds --regen

Machine B, measurement. Steps 2 through 5, in order:

.venv/bin/python diversity/labeling/sample.py items -n 300 <accepted.jsonl…> \
    --out diversity/labeling/out/sample_300.jsonl

.venv/bin/marimo edit diversity/labeling/author_notebook.py

.venv/bin/python diversity/labeling/judge_labels.py --sample diversity/labeling/out/sample_300.jsonl

.venv/bin/python diversity/labeling/kappa.py diversity/labeling/out/*.jsonl

.venv/bin/python diversity/labeling/hill.py diversity/labeling/out/judge_axes_*.jsonl
Two traps in that ladder Never use a glob for the sample inputs. output/frozen_19_*_accepted.jsonl also matches earlier runs and silently changes the sample.
Never run hill.py before kappa.py passes. That is the hinge, and running the steps out of order is the one way to produce a number the paper cannot use.

7. Retrieval check

Answer from memory before scrolling back.

The flat 13-role list scored κ = 0.550. What was the diagnosed cause?

The menu mixed several axes, so a practice owner buying from a lab was both "small business" and "B2B". The fix was structural: three orthogonal axes, each an ordered decision list.

The standing axis shows coverage 4 of 4 and effective 2.01. What does that pair mean?

Coverage is Hill q=0 — variety. Effective number is Hill q=1 — balance. Full variety with low balance is exactly a long tail: 71% "current", and "took over" twice in 209 items.

Which mechanism keeps one trigger family from taking over the seed corpus?

family_window in seedgen.py:71. The other three are real mechanisms, but they defend against duplicate seeds, domain pile-up, and entity convergence respectively.

One confusion pair holds 34% of an axis's disagreements. What is the pre-declared reading?

The 30% threshold is declared before the run in LABELING_DESIGN §6, so a pair above it is a construct finding about the menu — reported as such — not rater noise to be tuned away.

Read next

Primary source: Stirling, Diversity and its decomposition into variety, balance and disparity — arXiv:1902.09167. It is the frame the whole workstream stands on: variety = coverage, balance = the effective number, disparity = how far apart the categories are. Section 4 alone is enough. Read it and you will see why "34 of 120 cells" and "effective 20.05" are two different claims and both belong in the table.
